# IBM HR Attrition & Retention Analysis Dashboard

## Project Objective

Analyze employee attrition and retention patterns using Power BI to
understand where employee turnover is highest and which workforce
factors are associated with attrition.

The dashboard focuses on job role, department, overtime, business
travel, job satisfaction, salary, and years since last promotion.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** IBM HR Employee Attrition dataset

The dashboard analyzes employee-level HR information and presents the
results through an interactive Power BI executive dashboard.

## Business Recommendations

The following recommendations are **analyst recommendations based on the
dashboard indicators**, not statements directly provided by the source:

1.  **Focus retention efforts on high-attrition job roles** by
    investigating workload, compensation, career progression, and
    employee experience.
2.  **Review overtime patterns** and workload distribution, particularly
    in departments or roles showing elevated attrition.
3.  **Strengthen career progression programs** for employees with long
    periods since their last promotion.
4.  **Monitor employee satisfaction** and use targeted engagement
    initiatives for lower-satisfaction groups.
5.  **Review compensation structures** where salary differences may be
    contributing to retention challenges.
6.  **Analyze business-travel requirements** for roles with high travel
    exposure and consider workload or travel-support improvements.
7.  **Use dashboard segmentation regularly** so HR teams can monitor
    attrition by department, role, gender, marital status, overtime, and
    travel.
